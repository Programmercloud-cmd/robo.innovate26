API
engine/operational-engine.js exports OperationalEngine in a browser and the same
object through CommonJS. createRun(geometryManifest, options) computes a NEW,
serializable deterministic event trace. sample(run, time) reconstructs entities,
station counts, active resource owners, skip state and material accounting at
any time. The old replay is neither loaded nor translated.

The authoritative shared manifest is ../geometry-manifest.json. Pass its actual
SHA256 as options.manifestSha. Current manifest SHA256:

PROCESS IMPLEMENTATION
- Finite cart arrives from north on Z, retaining every source ID. A serialized
 40-brick claw lifts the top available layer, rotates the LOADED layer90degrees
 during its high transfer, and places it on the unchanged loading station.
 Main-belt travel and ten-brick row extraction precede single-brick feed and
 supported rotation into inspection. This simplifies36 simultaneous heads to
 one source claw and one row head per lane; simultaneous tooling clearance is
 not claimed. Rigid interpolation preserves layer geometry during rotation.
- Dual-view inspection is followed by reserved supported routes. Neighbor
 crossing occurs only at the intact upstream bridge X23.5125. The downstream
 fanout stays in the selected lane, outside the lower reject opening and cap.
 One brick owns each paired routing resource conservatively. A receiving slot
 and former capacity exist before release. There is no in-field waiting bank.
- Formation receives four rows of ten, closes the gaps on the powered field,
 then transfers a complete40-brick layer into positions36.835 and38.335.
 FIFO capacities and one shared portal per lane enforce occupied-zone holds.
 Retention gates remain lowered while the first layer position intersects them.
- The portal grips, raises and carries the complete layer to its descending
 well. Each placement is followed by an indexed0.069m lowering. Fourteen layers
 make exactly560 unique good bricks; completed wood datum0.224 finishes with
 runner bottom0.164 on the outfeed rollers. Pallet deck top is wood datum+0.060. Storage/handoff wood datum is0.060 on the floor.
- A single supported gantry transfer reserves a canonical shared-storage slot.
 The warehouse forklift follows the accepted working and cross aisles, with
 explicit loaded turns. One dedicated loader uses both exposed truck sides,
 placing each near row with a constant1.4m chassis-to-load-center offset.
 It crosses around the truck via the north connection before eastern loading.
 Cargo never jumps laterally from a stationary loader across a far truck row.
- Reject movement follows the chamfer, lower branch, common collector, incline
 and discharge head. Conservative pair/collector reservations prevent a second
 reject occupying a waiting branch. Dropped IDs become tracked bulk inventory
 in the installed skip, rather than fake whole-brick trajectories inside rubble.
 The300-brick exchange threshold is a SMALL DEMO LIMIT, not nominal10m3 capacity.
 Upstream release stops, admitted material clears, the head lifts0.5m before
 swinging west, body inventory is removed into a retained ledger, an empty body
 is positioned, then the raised head returns and lowers before release resumes.

PRESERVED COLOR RULES
Mixed lane recipes use (row+column+layer)%3, a valid version of the original
no-three-identical-neighbors requirement in all three grid directions.
The paired pure-color lane has Warm, Copper and Russet formers. Source inventory
colors are chosen by the deterministic commissioning request schedule, with
seeded reject decisions; this is an explicit demand-paced inventory assumption,
not a claim to reproduce old random-source yield or old output brick IDs.

ENTITY CONVENTIONS
Brick pose is geometric center [X,Y,Z,yaw]. Pallet pose is a wood datum with
support top+0.060 and runner bottom-0.060. WellDeck poseY is its support top.
Layer tool body bottom contacts the top of its held layer. Forklift pose is the
chassis center; load center is+1.4 localX. sample returns cargoPalletId and
forkTopY=wood datum+0.025 for fork contact under the deck in the two runner
channels. Fork top at0.085 on a ground-supported pallet clears the floor;
it does not treat the runner bottom as the fork-bearing interface. Truck-active starts at the accepted
bay1 pose and moves south only after loaders clear and timed stationary securing.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
Root's independent validation/audit-run.cjs reconstructs material ownership,
layer/pallet grouping, recipe constraints, transfer support and temporal zone
reservations. The current VM run passes30 check groups and four mutation-based
negative controls (see ../validation/). This is stronger than the engine's own
internal guards and was performed by a separate validation agent.

The engine does not solve rigid-body contact, friction, acceleration, claw force,
impact, dust, actual forklift load charts, steering/turning radius, hooklift
vehicle trajectories, structural spans or actuator drive design. Packs are
assumed unitized before handling. Fork/gantry representations are planning
interfaces. Storage/handoff resting support uses the factory floor atY0, with wood datum
0.060 and runner bottom0. This operational support-height assumption resolves
canonical support_y_m=null without changing any frozen slot or aisle geometry.
Outfeed retains wood datum0.224 on the0.164m rollers.
The source serialized schedule and initial portal hold are review scenarios.
The skip exchange is a logical isolated sequence, not simulated hooklift dynamics.
