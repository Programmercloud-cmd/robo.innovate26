INDEPENDENT VALIDATION SCOPE — UPDATED OPERATIONAL MODEL

The accepted geometry manifest is immutable. The audit derives product membership,
resource occupancy, temporal order and supported routes from the newly generated
run. The old revision37 replay is a read-only reference and is not a data source
for the new timeline.

Tests target the requested chain: receiving/routing, forty-brick compact layer,
two explicit roller-buffer positions, one portal per lane, fourteen ordered
layers per pallet, descending pallet support, completed-pallet outfeed, one
reserved gantry transfer into a canonical shared-storage slot. Resource conflicts
are checked from intervals, and buffers are also reconstructed from layer times.

Material conservation is identity-based. Every brick begins in finite source
inventory and stays in exactly one ledger state, including rejected material in
the skip and rejected material removed during exchange. Complete pallets have
exactly560 unique good IDs and fourteen forty-ID layers, with no reuse across
layers or pallets. Pure-colour packs and mixed three-dimensional no-three-equal
recipes are checked from actual record colours.

Supported route checks sample full horizontal brick footprint against the union
of the continuous upstream bridge and the lane decks. Cross-module motion must
use the supported upstream datum. This is a geometric support screen, not proof
of contact/friction/acceleration, cell actuation, structural capacity or industrial
safety qualification. Stationary gap handling and support datums need explicit
motion assumptions; the pallet contact top is wood actor origin +0.060m.

Skip exchange must stop upstream admission, drain material to the receiving body,
raise and stow the discharge head, then exchange the body. Full-body counts remain
in removed-reject accounting. The demonstration skip threshold is an explicit
simulation parameter rather than a claim of the actual10m3 body's capacity.

The warehouse slots, four access aisles, three cross aisles, gantry and adopted
OptionA geometry stay frozen. Warehouse/forklift motion needs route and resource
reservations; a path reservation is not qualification of a selected vehicle's
real swept path, load centre, traction or payload.

Negative controls intentionally duplicate a layer brick, overlap exclusive zone
owners, reverse pallet layer order and duplicate ledger material. Each injected
fault must be rejected by the independent auditor while the original run passes.
