INTERPRETATION
The current portfolio retains its demonstrated well change from Mixed to Warm after the first pallet.
Other cases only change the requested pallet recipes in memory; all physical scheduling remains native.
Two Mixed wells and one Warm well express available recipe destinations, not a guaranteed 67/33 order ratio.
Actual completed product counts are shown. Mixed may consume Warm too; it enforces no three equal
neighbours in any grid axis rather than a fixed one-third quota. Incomplete pallets/layers remain WIP,
not losses or shipped output. Rates are finite-batch kinematic event-model results, not physical benchmarks.
