ASSUMPTIONS
This is a controlled commissioning scenario, not a throughput forecast. Source
colors are demand-paced; one serialized source claw and row-extraction mechanism
represent the36-head unloader. A deliberate lane1 portal hold exercises buffers.
Skip exchange triggers at300 rejects as a small demo limit, not actual10m3 capacity.
Cart/tray/portal/forks/gantry interfaces and speeds are planning assumptions.
Well/outfeed wood datum.224 meets rollers.164; ground storage/handoff wood datum
.060 gives runner-bottom0. Forks bear on deck underside at wood datum+.025.
The engine does not solve friction, grip force, acceleration/contact dynamics,
brick impact, dust, exact vehicle/load charts, steering swept paths, hooklift
lifting trajectory, structural sizing or industrial machine safety qualification.
Motion assumptions are available inside the viewer.
