LIMITS / ASSUMPTIONS
This is a deterministic commissioning scenario with finite demand-paced source
colours, not a random factory-input or yield/throughput experiment. One serialized
forty-brick source claw and six row tools stand in for the industrial simultaneous
36-head unloading system. Speeds, singulation and indexing are authored review
kinematics. The initial portal hold deliberately demonstrates buffer backpressure.
The default bounded schedule lasts203,239.2811 model seconds; this deliberately
conservative serialized commissioning trace is not a factory cycle-time forecast.
Use the event jumps and timeline to inspect representative operations.

Support screens and sampled rigid transforms do not certify continuous contact,
friction, acceleration, gripper force, whole-machine collision or structural load.
The reject chute/incline and bulk-drop model does not solve brick breakage,
impact, settling or dust. The small skip trigger is a demonstration count, not
capacity qualification of the nominal10m3 body. Actual hooklift vehicle swept
paths, load limits, sensing and live interlocks remain engineering work.

Warehouse and truck handling assume unitized packs, an explicit load-center
convention and reserved journeys. Real vehicle footprint/turning-radius/fork
length/load-chart qualification remains open. Truck securing is a timed stationary
state; restraint hardware is not simulated. The second dispatch bay stays reserve.

The existing replay and Unreal project remain untouched. This delivery validates
logical/material invariants and authored supported paths for the identified new
run; it is not industrial safety certification or photoreal rendering.
